# Aash Brand Guidelines

## Brand idea

**Aash (عاش)** is a bilingual fitness and wellbeing companion. The name evokes
living; the experience should help people feel more capable and present in
everyday life, not judged for where they are starting.

**Promise:** Move well. Live fully.  
**Arabic expression:** تحرّك بصحة. عِش حياتك كاملة.

## Personality and voice

- Warm, capable, and encouraging; never shaming or overpromising.
- Use plain, action-oriented language and celebrate consistent effort.
- Arabic and English are first-class experiences. Keep Arabic right-to-left,
  use Cairo for Arabic and Inter for English, and avoid mixing directions
  inside the same text run.
- Introductions explain the personal value of a feature before asking for
  action. Let people skip them and keep them to one visit.

## Visual identity

- **Primary:** deep teal `#0B6254`; grounded and confident.
- **Deep primary:** `#08483F`; navigation and dark gradient endpoints.
- **Light primary:** `#54B99F`; dark-theme primary and quiet highlights.
- **Accent:** sunlit lime `#C8E66A`; use for progress, small highlights, and
  decorative details, not small white text on a button.
- **Canvas:** warm ivory `#F7F8F2`; raised cards are white.
- **Typography:** Inter for English and Cairo for Arabic. Use clear hierarchy,
  generous line height, and comfortable body sizes.
- **Shape:** soft but disciplined corners; cards and controls use the existing
  8/12/16 px radius scale.
- **Motion:** short, calm fade-and-lift transitions. Stagger onboarding
  elements lightly; avoid long, bouncing, or continuously distracting motion.

## Experience principles

1. Welcome people in their chosen language before asking them to register.
2. Explain coaching, workouts, nutrition, and the store on first visit only.
3. Keep screen changes predictable and brief; preserve state when users go back.
4. Use brand colors semantically and maintain readable contrast in light and
   dark themes.
5. Prefer progress and reassurance to pressure, especially in fitness,
   nutrition, and subscription flows.
6. Keep the home feed focused on progress and timely information; primary
   features belong in the bottom navigation, not duplicated launch cards.
7. Make account sections visible and scannable so profile, health,
   subscription, notifications, and settings are easy to find.
